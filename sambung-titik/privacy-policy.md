# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Sambung Titik app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi (collectively, the "Application"). Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Sambung Titik is an educational app that helps young children (from preschool to grade 3 of elementary school) practice writing letters, numbers, and words by connecting dots, together with simple word and math exercises. The Application does not require an account, does not ask for an email address, phone number, or location, and does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

**Summary**

*   All progress and profiles are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the Service Provider or to any third party.
*   No email address, account, or registration is needed.
*   A profile consists of a nickname, a picture (avatar), and an optional school grade. We recommend using a nickname rather than a child's full name.
*   The Application uses the internet only to download public learning content. It does not upload any of your data.

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application does not collect personally identifiable information, and the Service Provider does not receive any data about how you use the Application.

The Application does not request any device permission other than internet access, which is used only to download public learning content (see below). It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, location, or advertising identifier.

**Information Stored on Your Device**

The Application stores the following information only in the Application's private storage on your device, in order to provide its features:

*   **Player profiles:** a nickname (up to 12 characters) and a picture (avatar) chosen for each child, the date the profile was created and last played, and an optional school grade (1, 2, or 3) entered by a teacher or parent to suggest matching exercises. Up to 30 profiles can be created on one device.
*   **Learning progress:** completed stages, stars, unlocked levels, and results of word and math exercises (such as the number of times played and the number of correct and incorrect answers).
*   **Writing practice data:** statistics about letters practiced (such as writing direction and neatness) and the child's handwriting strokes, kept so that the child can look at earlier writing in "Buku Tulisanku" (My Writing Book). Handwriting is stored as lines drawn on the screen, not as photographs.
*   **Activity history:** the type, score, stars, and date and time of each completed session, and a daily summary used for the star board.
*   **Settings:** sound preferences and the profile last used.
*   **Teacher menu PIN:** if a PIN is set to protect the teacher menu, only a salted, one-way hash of the PIN is stored, not the PIN itself.
*   **Downloaded learning content:** word lists, letter shapes, and similar public content.

This information is not transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party. The Application turns off Android's automatic app backup, so this data is not included in your device or cloud backups.

**Content Downloads**

To keep learning content up to date, the Application periodically downloads public content files over HTTPS (when the Application starts and at most once every 6 hours afterwards). These files are served by public content delivery networks (jsDelivr, Statically, and GitHub via raw.githubusercontent.com). The Application only downloads files from these services; it does not upload any of your data to them. As with any internet request, these services may automatically receive and log technical information, such as:

*   Your device's Internet Protocol address
*   The file requested and the time and date of the request
*   Basic request information sent by the network library (for example, the HTTP client name and version)

This information is processed by those services under their own privacy policies, and the Service Provider does not have access to it. If the download fails or the device is offline, the Application keeps working with the content bundled in the Application or downloaded earlier.

**Text-to-Speech**

The Application reads letters, words, and instructions aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Only learning content (such as letters, words, and numbers) is passed to the engine; no profile or personal information is sent. The text-to-speech engine is provided by your device manufacturer or another provider you have chosen, and its handling of data is governed by that provider's privacy policy.

**Cookies and tracking technologies**

The Application does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, pixels, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or similar tracking technologies.

**Your Rights and Deleting Data**

Because the Service Provider does not collect or hold personal data from the Application, all data described above remains under your control on your device. You can:

*   delete a single profile and all of its data from the teacher menu in the Application;
*   delete all data stored by the Application by clearing the Application's storage in your device settings; or
*   delete everything by uninstalling the Application.

If you have any questions about your rights, contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com.

**Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)**

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not collect, sell, or share personal information through the Application. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights or ask questions about them, contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com.

**Third Party Access**

The Service Provider does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. The only third-party services the Application communicates with are the content delivery networks and the device text-to-speech engine described above, which receive only the technical information needed to deliver content or speech.

**International Data Transfers**

The content delivery networks used by the Application may operate servers in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Technical information such as your IP address may therefore be processed in those countries when the Application downloads content. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those services are responsible for applying appropriate mechanisms, such as:

*   Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
*   Adequacy decisions or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms

Data protection laws in other countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

The Service Provider may disclose information it holds, such as the contents of messages you send to the Service Provider by email:

*   as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
*   when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop the Application from downloading content by disconnecting your device from the internet while using it; the Application will continue to work with the content already available on the device. You can stop all processing by uninstalling the Application, which also deletes all data the Application has stored on your device.

**Data Retention Policy**

*   Data stored on your device: Retained on your device until you delete a profile, clear the Application's storage, or uninstall the Application. Because the data is not backed up, it cannot be restored once deleted or if you change or lose your device.
*   Data processed by content delivery networks: Retained by those services according to their own privacy policies. The Service Provider does not have access to it.
*   Emails you send to the Service Provider: Retained only as long as needed to respond to your request, unless longer retention is required by law.

**Children**

The Application is designed for young children, including children under the age of 13. The Application does not require an account, does not ask for an email address, phone number, or location, does not show advertising, does not contain links that take a child out of the Application, does not allow children to communicate with other people, and does not use analytics or tracking technologies. Profile information (a nickname, a picture, and an optional school grade) stays on the device and is never sent to the Service Provider. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

We encourage parents, guardians, and teachers to supervise children's use of the Application and to use a nickname instead of a child's full name when creating a profile. The optional teacher menu is protected by a PIN that a parent or teacher can set, so that children cannot change settings or delete profiles by accident. If you have reason to believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the Service Provider, for example by email, please contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com so that they will be able to take the necessary actions, including deleting that information.

**Security**

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Application downloads content only over encrypted HTTPS connections and verifies downloaded files before using them. Data stored by the Application is kept in the Application's private storage on your device, which is protected by your device's operating system, and the teacher menu PIN is stored only as a salted hash.

If a data breach occurs that affects your personal data held by the Service Provider, the Service Provider will notify you in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including, where required, providing information about the nature of the breach and the steps being taken to address it. Because the Application stores profile data only on your device, the Service Provider does not hold such data.
