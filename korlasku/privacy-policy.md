# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the KorlasKu app for Android devices (package id.co.kct.korlasku), together with any related services operated by Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi (collectively, the "Application"). Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

KorlasKu is an offline bookkeeping app for school class cash, made for class treasurers, homeroom teachers, and teachers in Indonesia. The Application does not require an account, has no server, and does not request the internet permission, so it makes no network requests. It contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases.

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application does not collect personal information for the Service Provider, and the Service Provider does not receive any data you enter or any data about how you use the Application. Because the Application has no internet access, nothing leaves it for the Service Provider or any third party unless you share it yourself, as described under "When Data Leaves Your Device".

**Information Stored on Your Device**

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the Application's own local SQLite database:

*   School details: school name, address, signatory, and an optional school logo
*   Classes and students: student names, student numbers, notes, and parent or guardian names and phone numbers
*   Dues, and cash in and cash out entries with their categories and notes, including an optional receipt photo for each entry
*   Application settings and the reminder schedules you set

Android cloud auto-backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the Application. Its data is therefore not copied to your Google account backup and is not moved to a new device by Android's transfer tools. To move your data, use the Application's own backup export described below.

**Device Permissions**

The Application requests only these Android permissions:

*   Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), to show the dues and bookkeeping reminders you schedule
*   Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), to reschedule those local reminders after the device restarts
*   Vibration (VIBRATE), so reminders can vibrate the device

The Application does not hold the camera, contacts, or internet permissions. When you add a receipt photo or school logo, it opens your device's camera app or the system photo picker, and only the image you take or pick is saved. When you choose a parent's phone number, it opens the Android contact picker once, and the Application receives only the one contact you select. It does not read your contact list.

**When Data Leaves Your Device**

Data leaves your device only when you choose to send it:

*   When you save, print, or share a PDF report through the Android share sheet or print service
*   When you export a ZIP backup file and save or share it
*   When you send a payment reminder: the Application opens WhatsApp through a wa.me link with the phone number and message filled in, and you review the text and decide whether to send it

Once shared, the data is handled by the app or service you chose (for example WhatsApp, a file storage app, or a print service), under that provider's own privacy policy.

**Cookies and tracking technologies**

The Application does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, pixels, analytics SDKs, or similar tracking technologies.

**Your Rights**

Because the Service Provider does not collect or hold personal data from the Application, all data described above remains under your control on your device. You can edit or delete students, entries, and other records in the Application at any time, or delete all Application data by clearing the Application's storage or uninstalling the Application. If you have any questions about your rights, contact the Service Provider at team@kct.co.id.

**Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)**

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not collect, sell, or share personal information through the Application. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights or ask questions about them, contact the Service Provider at team@kct.co.id.

**Third Party Access**

The Service Provider does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. The Application does not communicate with any third-party service. Third parties receive data only when you share a PDF report, backup file, or WhatsApp message yourself.

**International Data Transfers**

The Application does not transfer data to the Service Provider or to servers in any country, because it makes no network requests. If you share a report, backup file, or message through another app or service, that provider may process the data in other countries under its own privacy policy.

The Service Provider may disclose information it holds, such as the contents of messages you send to the Service Provider by email:

*   as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
*   when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop reminder notifications by turning off the Application's notification permission in Android settings. You can stop all processing by uninstalling the Application, which also deletes all data the Application has stored on your device.

**Data Retention Policy**

*   Data stored on your device: Retained until you delete it in the Application, clear the Application's storage, or uninstall the Application. Clearing storage or uninstalling deletes all of it.
*   PDF reports and backup files you exported: Stored outside the Application, so keeping or deleting them is your responsibility. Uninstalling the Application does not delete them.
*   Emails you send to the Service Provider: Retained only as long as needed to respond to your request, unless longer retention is required by law.

**Children**

The Application is intended for adults (class treasurers, homeroom teachers, and teachers) and is not directed at children. The Application does not ask children for personal information, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

Because you may record data about students who are minors, such as their names and their parents' phone numbers, you are responsible for making sure you have a lawful basis for that record-keeping, including obtaining consent from parents or guardians where required by Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP). If you have reason to believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the Service Provider, for example by email, please contact the Service Provider at team@kct.co.id so that they will be able to take the necessary actions, including deleting that information.

**Security**

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Data stored by the Application is kept in the Application's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read and which is protected by your device's operating system.

Exported ZIP backup files are not encrypted. Anyone who has the file can read its contents, including student names and parent phone numbers. Keep backup files somewhere only you can access, and do not share them in groups or with people who do not need them.

**Data Breach Notification**

If a data breach occurs that affects your personal data held by the Service Provider, the Service Provider will notify you in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including, where required, providing information about the nature of the breach and the steps being taken to address it.

**Changes**

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when new features are added to the Application. The Service Provider will notify you of material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy with an effective date. Where required by law, the Service Provider will seek your consent to material changes before they take effect.

Previous versions of this Privacy Policy will be maintained and made available upon request by contacting the Service Provider at team@kct.co.id.

By using the Application, you acknowledge the processing described in this Privacy Policy. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the Application, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about the practices, please contact the Service Provider via email at team@kct.co.id.
