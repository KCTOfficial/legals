# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Mediany app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi (collectively, the "Application"). Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Mediany is a media player and converter. It plays videos and music, shows photos, and converts, compresses, repairs, and inspects media files. All of this happens on your device. The Application does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, does not have permission to access the internet, and does not contain advertising, analytics, or crash reporting SDKs.

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application does not collect personally identifiable information, and the Service Provider does not receive any data about you or about how you use the Application. The Application does not request the Android internet permission, so it is not able to send your files or any other information from your device to the Service Provider or to anyone else.

The app store from which you download the Application (for example, Google Play) may collect information about your installation and, if you have allowed it in your device settings, technical diagnostics such as crash reports. That information is collected and processed by the app store provider under its own privacy policy. The Service Provider may see aggregated, non-identifying statistics from it, such as the number of installations and crash rates.

**Your Media Files**

The Application accesses the videos, music, and photos on your device only to show them to you, to play them, and to process the files you choose. Your files are read and processed on your device and are never uploaded.

*   Converted, compressed, and repaired files are saved as new files in the Mediany folders on your device (Movies/Mediany, Music/Mediany, and Pictures/Mediany). The original files are never changed or deleted by the Application.
*   When you open or share a file to the Application from another app, the Application receives read access to that file only, as granted by Android.
*   When you choose a file with "Browse other files" (the Android file picker), the Application may keep read access to that file, so that a queued job, or retrying a job later, can still read it. This access is limited to the files you picked and ends when you uninstall the Application.

Files saved in the Mediany folders are ordinary files in your device's shared storage, so other apps that you allow to access your media can also see them.

**Information Stored on Your Device**

The Application stores the following information only in its private storage on your device, in order to provide its features:

*   The playback position of videos you have watched, so that you can resume where you stopped
*   A history of your processing jobs (conversions, compressions, and repairs), consisting of the name of each input file and a reference to it, the type of job and its settings, its status and any error, a reference to the output file, the date and time it finished, and, for repairs, the damage report shown to you. The history keeps up to the 200 most recent finished jobs
*   Temporary files used while processing, and a cache of thumbnails, which Android may clear at any time

This information is not transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party. The Application is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so this information is not copied into your device backups.

**Permissions**

The Application asks only for the permissions it needs:

*   Photos, videos, and music (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, and READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED; storage access on Android 12 and earlier): to show your media in the Application and to read the files you choose to play or process. On Android 14 and later you can allow access to selected photos and videos only.
*   Media location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): requested only when you allow it in Media info, so that the Application can show whether a photo or video contains GPS location. The location is only shown to you.
*   Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show processing progress and results, and music playback controls.
*   Foreground services (media playback, media processing, and data sync): to keep music playing and to keep processing files after you leave the Application.

You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. You can still choose individual files with "Browse other files" without granting media access.

**Photo and Video Metadata**

Media files can contain metadata, such as camera details, dates, and GPS location. Media info shows this metadata to you on your device. When converting, you can turn on "Remove metadata" so that the new file does not include tags, camera details, or location. Converted files otherwise keep the metadata of the original file.

**Cookies and tracking technologies**

The Application does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, pixels, analytics SDKs, or similar tracking technologies.

**Your Rights**

Because the Service Provider does not collect or hold personal data from the Application, all data described above remains under your control on your device. You can delete the processing history at any time with "Clear history" on the Home screen, delete all Application data by clearing the Application's storage, or uninstall the Application. Files the Application has saved in the Mediany folders stay on your device until you delete them. If you have any questions about your rights, contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com.

**Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)**

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not collect, sell, or share personal information through the Application. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights or ask questions about them, contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com.

**Third Party Access**

The Service Provider does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. The Application includes open-source components, such as FFmpeg, that run entirely inside the Application on your device; they do not send data anywhere. When you open an output file in another app, that app's handling of the file is governed by its own privacy policy.

**International Data Transfers**

The Application does not transfer your data to any country, because it does not send data off your device. If you contact the Service Provider by email, your message is processed by the email provider used by you and by the Service Provider, which may store it in countries outside your country of residence.

The Service Provider may disclose information it holds, such as the contents of messages you send to the Service Provider by email:

*   as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
*   when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop the Application from accessing your media by denying or revoking its media permissions in your device settings, and you can turn off its notifications at any time. You can stop all processing by uninstalling the Application, which also deletes all data the Application has stored in its private storage. Files saved in the Mediany folders are not deleted when you uninstall the Application.

**Data Retention Policy**

*   Playback positions: Retained on your device until you clear the Application's storage or uninstall the Application.
*   Processing history: Retained on your device until you use "Clear history", until it is replaced by newer jobs beyond the 200 most recent, or until you clear the Application's storage or uninstall the Application. "Clear finished" in the Queue only hides finished jobs from the queue; they stay in the history.
*   Temporary files and thumbnails: Retained only as long as needed, and may be cleared by Android at any time.
*   Files saved in the Mediany folders: Retained until you delete them.
*   Emails you send to the Service Provider: Retained only as long as needed to respond to your request, unless longer retention is required by law.

**Children**

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. The Application does not collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children, and does not show advertising or use analytics or tracking technologies.

If you have reason to believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the Service Provider, for example by email, please contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com so that they will be able to take the necessary actions, including deleting that information.

**Security**

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application has no internet permission, your files and data cannot be transmitted by the Application. Data stored by the Application is kept in the Application's private storage on your device, which is protected by your device's operating system. Media files are processed in a separate process of the Application, so that a damaged or malformed file cannot affect the rest of the Application.

**Data Breach Notification**

If a data breach occurs that affects your personal data held by the Service Provider, the Service Provider will notify you in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including, where required, providing information about the nature of the breach and the steps being taken to address it.
