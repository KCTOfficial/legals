# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Mealora app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi (collectively, the "Application"). Kreasindo Cipta Teknologi is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Mealora is a food diary and nutrition tracking app that helps you log meals, track your weight, and follow nutrition targets. The Application does not require an account, does not ask for your email address, phone number, or location, and does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application does not require you to register, and the Service Provider does not receive your profile, food diary, or weight history. That information stays on your device (see "Information Stored on Your Device").

Some optional features send limited data over the internet, and only when you use them:

*   When you choose to have the Application read a nutrition label or recognize food in a photo, that photo is sent over HTTPS to a server operated by the Service Provider, which forwards it to the Google Gemini API for processing and returns the result to your device. By default, the Application asks for your permission each time before an image is sent; you can change this in the Application's "Privacy & local data" settings. The Service Provider's server does not store the image or the result.
*   When you describe a meal by voice, your speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service of your device (provided by Google on Android or Apple on iOS). Only the resulting text transcript is sent to the Service Provider's server and forwarded to the Google Gemini API to identify foods and portions. The Service Provider never receives audio recordings, and the transcript is not stored.
*   When you search for a packaged product or scan a barcode, the barcode number or search keywords are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up product information. No personal information is included.
*   The Application may contact the Service Provider's server to check whether a newer version is available. This request contains no personal information.

As with any internet request, the Service Provider's server and the third-party services above may automatically receive technical information, such as:

*   Your device's Internet Protocol address
*   The time and date of the request
*   Basic request information sent by the network library (for example, the HTTP client name and version)

The Service Provider uses this information only to deliver responses and to enforce usage limits that protect the service from abuse. The Service Provider's server logs only technical metadata (such as the endpoint, response status, and duration) and does not log images, transcripts, or results.

If you decline to send an image, or if the device is offline, you can still enter food details manually. The Application can also read label text directly on your device using on-device text recognition, without sending the image anywhere.

**Information Stored on Your Device**

The Application stores the following information only in local storage on your device, in order to provide its features:

*   Your profile: nickname, date of birth, height, weight, biological sex used for calculations, and activity level, used to calculate your energy and nutrition targets
*   Your weight history and any notes you add
*   Your food diary: foods, portions, meal times, nutrition values, and recipes you create
*   Programs you follow and your progress in them
*   Source photos of nutrition labels or meals, only if you choose to keep them in settings (by default, photos are deleted after the entry is logged)
*   Your preferences, such as display settings, reminders, image upload consent settings, and time zone

Some of this information, such as weight and food intake, may be considered health-related data. This information is not transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party. If backup is enabled in your device settings, your operating system may include this locally stored data in your device backup (for example, to your Google or Apple account); such backups are governed by your backup provider's privacy policy and settings.

**Device Permissions**

The Application may request the following permissions, only when the related feature is used:

*   Camera: to scan barcodes and photograph nutrition labels or meals
*   Microphone: for voice food input
*   Notifications: for meal and program reminders, including rescheduling reminders after your device restarts

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. The rest of the Application continues to work without them.

**Cookies and tracking technologies**

The Application does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, pixels, analytics SDKs, or similar tracking technologies.

**Your Rights**

Because your profile, food diary, and weight history are stored only on your device, they remain under your control. You can view and correct them in the Application, withdraw consent for image uploads in the Application's "Privacy & local data" settings, and delete all Application data by clearing the Application's storage or uninstalling the Application. If you have any questions about your rights, contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com.

**Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)**

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not sell or share personal information through the Application. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights or ask questions about them, contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com.

**Third Party Access**

The Service Provider does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising. The Application relies on the following third-party services, which receive only the data needed for the feature you use:

*   [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), which processes photos and text transcripts sent through the Service Provider's server
*   [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy), which receives barcode numbers and search keywords
*   Your device's speech recognition service (Google or Apple), which processes your voice when you use voice input

**International Data Transfers**

The third-party services above may operate servers in countries outside your country of residence, including outside Indonesia and the European Economic Area (EEA). Photos, text transcripts, and technical information may therefore be processed in those countries when you use the related features. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those services are responsible for applying appropriate mechanisms, such as:

*   Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
*   Adequacy decisions or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms

Data protection laws in other countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

The Service Provider may disclose information it holds, such as the contents of messages you send to the Service Provider by email:

*   as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
*   when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can avoid sending any data to the Service Provider or third parties by not using the online features (photo reading, voice input, and product search), or by keeping the image upload setting on "Ask every time" and declining. You can stop all processing by uninstalling the Application, which also deletes all data the Application has stored on your device.

**Data Retention Policy**

*   Data stored on your device: Retained on your device until you delete it in the Application, clear the Application's storage, or uninstall the Application.
*   Photos and transcripts sent for processing: Not retained by the Service Provider after the result is returned to your device. Processing by the Google Gemini API is governed by Google's terms.
*   Data processed by Open Food Facts and device speech services: Retained by those services according to their own privacy policies. The Service Provider does not have access to it.
*   Emails you send to the Service Provider: Retained only as long as needed to respond to your request, unless longer retention is required by law.

**Children**

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13, and its nutrition targets are designed for adults. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you have reason to believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the Service Provider, for example by email, please contact the Service Provider at kctapps@gmail.com so that they will be able to take the necessary actions, including deleting that information.

**Health Disclaimer**

Nutrition information and targets in the Application are provided for general information only and are not medical advice. Results read by AI from photos or voice may be inaccurate; please review them before saving. Consult a doctor or a qualified nutritionist about your health conditions.

**Security**

The Application communicates with online services only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and AI service keys are kept on the Service Provider's server rather than inside the Application. Data stored by the Application is kept in the Application's private storage on your device, which is protected by your device's operating system. Because the Service Provider does not keep a copy of your data, data lost by uninstalling the Application or losing your device cannot be recovered by the Service Provider.
